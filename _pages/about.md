Hello, I’m Shun!   

I am currently a PhD student and pre-doctoral fellow (FPI) at Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC), Barcelona, Spain. I am very fortunate to be part of the [Research Group of Control, Data, and Artificial Intelligence (CoDAlab)](https://codalab.upc.edu/en) and to be advised by [Prof. Francesc Pozo](https://futur.upc.edu/FrancescPozoMontero) and [Prof. Yolanda Vidal](https://futur.upc.edu/YolandaVidalSegui). My PhD research focuses on AI strategies for predictive maintenance and early damage detection, Structural Health Monitoring and Condition Monitoring for wind turbines. From February to June 2026, I was a visiting researcher at [University College Dublin (UCD)](https://www.ucd.ie/), working with [Prof. Abdollah Malekjafarian](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=k0pgT8oAAAAJ&hl=en) on structural health monitoring for wind turbine blades.

Before pursuing my PhD, I received my master's degree (with distinction) from the [School of Aeronautics](http://hangkong.nwpu.edu.cn/), [Northwestern Polytechnical University](http://www.nwpu.edu.cn/) (NPU), Xi'an, China in 2023, supervised by [Prof. Yongbo Li](https://teacher.nwpu.edu.cn/8FA4308A7D6048AA9C1C6F7AFA7D9225.html). I received the B.Eng. degree in Aircraft Control and Information Engineering from NPU in 2020.  

My research interests include: 
- Condition monitoring
- Structural health monitoring
- Wind turbines
- Fault diagnosis and fault detection
- Machine learning / Artificial intelligence
- Signal processing
- Complexity / Entropy
